# PROJECT_AUDIT — Walk in Covenant (与约同行), David chapter

**Scope:** the repository at `7b23ead` (the merge of the pass-3 fixes), read in
full. Everything below is either counted out of the source or quoted from it.
Nothing here is inferred from a previous report, and nothing here is a claim to
have *run* the game: this sandbox has no Godot binary, no display, no sound card
and no phone. Execution happens in CI (`tests/run_all.gd`, `tests/smoke.gd`,
`tests/deep.gd`) on GitHub Actions, and the build deploys to GitHub Pages.

## 2. Architecture, in one paragraph

`Main.gd` (1,450 lines) is the chapter spine: it owns the `Act` enum, region
lifecycle, the mission runner wiring, the David-only mechanics (drill, errand,
armour, brook, duel) and the ending. `Region.gd` builds a map from JSON and owns
the player, the NPCs and the dialogue box. `RegionModel.gd` (325) is the pure
data model — grid, collision, A* — and knows nothing about scenes, which is what
lets the content lint instantiate it headless. `MissionRunner.gd` is a generic
state machine over a list of `{id, act, objective, complete, rewards}` dicts.
A region may name a `chapter_script` (`scripts/campaigns/Bethlehem.gd`, 220) and
then owns its own story beats through four doors: `dialogue`, `set_paused`,
`model`, `GameState`. Autoloads: `GameState` (save), `I18n`, `AudioDirector`,
`EventBus`.

**This seam works.** A second Bible story was added without `Main` learning its
name, and the content lint enforces the contract for any region file.

## 3. Progression as it actually plays

`elah` — The Valley of Elah (1 Samuel 17):

1. Prologue (5 lines) → briefing (5) → place card.
2. Corin the scribe: the witness's rule, and an escort.
3. Three camp people, one verse each (Deut 20:3, Ps 23:1, 1 Sam 16:7).
4. First sight of the giant (scripted camera shot) → the line → 17:10, 17:11.
5. **BECOME** — the player is David, three days earlier.
6. Sling drill (3 jars) → Jesse's errand (3 provisions) → the brothers → Saul →
   the armour → the brook (5 of 9 stones by hand) → the line → **the duel**
   (dodge + throw, 3 stones) → 17:47 → aftermath with Corin → ending.
7. Free roam; the south road opens.

`bethlehem` — 1 Samuel 16:1–13: the elder at the gate → the procession of the
seven sons (16:7, 16:10, 16:11) → fetch the youngest from the flock → the
anointing (16:12, 16:13) → the road home.

## 4. Assets and text, honestly

- **Chinese** is authored, not translated: `elah.json` and `bethlehem.json` carry
  hand-written `zh` for every line, and the register is right — short sentences,
  concrete nouns, no devotional vocabulary. It is the strongest thing here.
- **Scripture** is verbatim KJV + 和合本, always marked by a citation line above
  the quotation, always in 「」 / “ ”. `_check_verses()` refuses a quotation whose
  marks do not balance. This is enforced, not intended.
- **Art** is coherent in scale (16 px grid, 32×64 idle / 64×64 walk) but thin:
  one village tileset, no animals, no weather, no interiors, no lighting.
- **Audio** exists but has never been heard by anyone on this project.

## 5. What was wrong when this audit started

32 defects had already been found and fixed in passes 1–3 (`docs/BUGS.md`), the
last ten by this same method — reading the code against its own data. This pass
found five more, and they are recorded in `docs/BUG_TRACKER.md`. The pattern
that produced most of them is worth stating plainly:

> **A state is written in one place and read in another, and nothing owns the
> contract between them.** Props declared `solid: false` and collided anyway; a
> save with the wrong types crashed the boot; a music key was recorded while
> muted and never loaded; the restored stone count never reached the HUD; a
> language signal had no subscribers. Every one is the same shape.

## 7. The three most serious remaining weaknesses (2026-11-05 refresh)

1. **Nothing has been verified on a real device, in a browser export, or by
   ear.** Fourteen *rendered engine frames* have now been decoded and looked at,
   with committed baselines — see `docs/VISUAL-TESTING.md` — but that is
   software GL on a CI runner, not a phone in two aspect ratios, not a sound
   card, not a thumb, and not the shipped WebAssembly export. Every
   presentation score is still capped by this. `docs/DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md`
   remains the untouched script.
2. **The camp has never been seen close up.** `13_duel` and `14_aftermath`
   now capture the climax and the empty ridge, and three review defects were
   found in those very frames — good evidence that the beats nobody has
   captured still hold defects. Audio ducking, crossfades and levels are
   asserted as state, never heard.
3. **Small type on a small logical screen.** 9–10 px Chinese at 320×180 has
   been read in upscaled CI PNGs, and the optional journal rows were brightened
   from review — but on a real handset in 4:3 and 16:9 that judgement is still
   a guess, and the journal button remains 26×17 logical units (O-3).

**Closed this session (were §7 items 1 and 2 before the refresh):**

- **1 Samuel 16 was optional and sat after 1 Samuel 17.** The south road now
  opens on `elah_met_the_line`, a `bethlehem_call` mission sits before
  `shepherd_drill`, and BECOME fires on the walk back. Version-1 saves get a
  `legacy_spine` migration. `smoke.gd` plays Bethlehem before
  `act_id() == "shepherd"` and green on CI — see `docs/ITERATION.md` §2.
- **Act 1 had no playable shepherd life.** The slope now has a fold, standing
  sheep, three strays to bring in, a hired boy who answers to the act, an
  optional broken-hurdle discovery and a family exchange that leads into the
  errand — see `docs/ITERATION.md` §3.
- **LIVING WORLD pass:** the reading wall, the static flock, silent NPCs and
  the fixed-time-of-day are all closed; 12 frames reviewed, three defects
  fixed from them. See `docs/ITERATION.md` §8.

## 8. What must not be touched

The mission runner, the campaign-script seam, the region-data contract, the
verse-integrity rules, `TapGate` arbitration, the save's tmp→rename→`.bak`
write, and the three test suites. Each of them is load-bearing and each has
already survived a defect that tried to break it.

"Not touched" means **not rewritten or weakened**. This session *extended* the
contracts where the milestone required it: one new mission, one new pickup
kind, one new save version, a Scripture-marker gate, a flock-resume regression,
and a fourth (visual) suite. No existing assertion was deleted; the two that
were replaced asserted the wrong thing for the corrected chronology, and both
replacements are stronger. `tests/deep.gd`'s `MIN_CHECKS = 100` floor still
holds (134 checks on the last green run). The LIVING WORLD pass added checks
rather than removing them: `_check_bubbles()`, `_check_living_flock()`, cold
boot, live-position herding, a logical 4:3 assertion and a twelfth frame.
